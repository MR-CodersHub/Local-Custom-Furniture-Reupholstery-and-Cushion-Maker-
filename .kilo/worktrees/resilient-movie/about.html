Our Story & Heritage

Thirty-five years of dedication to the ancient art of upholstery — guided by passion, precision and an unwavering commitment to quality.

Founded 1987

A Workshop Where Every Piece Has a Story

Thomas Mercer started Craftwork Atelier with a single workbench, a set of inherited tools, and an unyielding belief that quality furniture should be restored rather than replaced. What began as a one-man studio in a converted Notting Hill mews has grown into London's most respected bespoke upholstery atelier.

Today, three generations of the Mercer family work alongside a team of twelve specialist craftspeople — each trained in traditional hand-tied spring techniques, pattern matching, and heritage fabric handling that many modern upholsterers have never learned.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Four principles that guide every decision we make, every stitch we place and every client relationship we cherish.

Uncompromising Craft

We refuse to cut corners. Traditional hand-tying, double-stitched edges and meticulous pattern matching define every piece that leaves our studio.

Honest Transparency

Clear pricing, realistic timelines and honest assessments. We never oversell, never surprise with hidden costs and always deliver what we promise.

Sustainable Practice

Restoration is the ultimate form of sustainability. We extend furniture life by decades, partner with eco-certified fabric mills and minimise workshop waste.

Client Partnership

Your furniture, your vision. We listen first, advise second and always collaborate closely to ensure the finished piece exceeds your expectations.

The Atelier

Meet the Artisans

Each member of our team has been trained in both traditional and contemporary upholstery techniques — bringing a unique perspective to every commission.

Thomas Mercer
Founder & Master Upholsterer

With over 40 years in the craft, Thomas trained under the legendary Roy Easton before establishing Craftwork Atelier. He specialises in Victorian and Edwardian restoration.

Elena Mercer
Creative Director & Fabric Specialist

Thomas's daughter, Elena brings a trained designer's eye to fabric selection and custom headboard creation. She curates all new fabric arrivals from European mills.

Marcus Webb
Senior Upholsterer & Cushion Specialist

Fifteen years of precision cushion making and deep-buttoning expertise. Marcus heads our outdoor and commercial cushion department with remarkable consistency.

Our History

35 Years of
Milestones

From a single workbench to London's most trusted upholstery atelier — a journey built on craft, family and community.

1987
The Beginning

Thomas Mercer opens a small one-man studio in a Notting Hill mews, focusing on Victorian armchair restoration.

1995
Studio Expansion

Growing demand leads to a larger workshop space and the hiring of two additional craftspeople. Custom cushion making is added to the service offering.

2003
Design Partnership

Craftwork Atelier becomes the preferred upholstery partner for leading interior designers across West London.

2012
Second Generation

Elena Mercer joins the business, bringing a design-school trained perspective and launching the bespoke headboard collection.

2018
Award Recognition

Named 'Best Upholstery Studio in London' by the British Interior Design Association. Workshop team grows to twelve artisans.

2025
Heritage Preserved

Celebrating 35 years and over 4,800 pieces transformed — with the same passion, precision and integrity as day one.
