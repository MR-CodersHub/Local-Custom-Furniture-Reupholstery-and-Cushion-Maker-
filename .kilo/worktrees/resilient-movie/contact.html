Quick Answers

Before You Get in Touch

How quickly can you provide a quote?+
We aim to respond to all enquiries within 24 hours on business days. For photo quotes we can usually turn these around the same day. For complex projects a studio visit is recommended.
Do I need an appointment to visit?+
Yes, we operate by appointment to ensure each client receives dedicated attention. You can book via phone, email or the contact form above. Saturday appointments are very popular — book ahead!
What areas do you serve?+
We serve all areas within the M25 with our free collection and delivery service. For clients further afield, we can arrange courier collection at cost price and provide remote fabric consultation.
Can I get fabric samples before committing?+
Absolutely. We offer a complimentary fabric sample service — simply tell us your colour preferences and style direction and we'll send a curated selection directly to your door. No commitment required.
