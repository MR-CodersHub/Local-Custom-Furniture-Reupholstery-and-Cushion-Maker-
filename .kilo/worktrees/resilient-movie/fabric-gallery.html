Fabric Advice

Choosing the Right Fabric

Not all fabrics are suitable for all furniture. Our artisans consider rub count (durability), weave structure, cleaning properties and aesthetics to help you find the perfect match.

High Traffic Areas
Choose fabrics with 30,000+ rub count. Performance weaves, wool blends and quality velvets perform best.
Outdoor Use
Sunbrella and marine-grade fabrics offer UV resistance, mould prevention and water repellency.
Statement Pieces
Silk-blend jacquards, patterned velvets and leather create genuine focal points in any room.
