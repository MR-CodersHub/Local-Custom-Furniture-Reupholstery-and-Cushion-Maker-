35 Years of Heritage

Craft Runs
Deep in Our DNA

Since Thomas Mercer opened the atelier in 1987, every piece that has passed through our doors has been treated with the same reverence, skill and passion. Three generations of one family — twelve master artisans — one unwavering standard.

We believe that truly excellent furniture is never really finished — it simply moves to its next chapter. Our role is to make that chapter extraordinary.
