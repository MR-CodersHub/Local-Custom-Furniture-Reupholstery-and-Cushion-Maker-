Pricing FAQ

Your Pricing Questions Answered

Are quotes fixed-price?+
Yes. Once we have assessed your piece and you have confirmed your fabric choice, we provide a fixed-price quote. There will never be unexpected additions to your invoice unless you request additional work during the process.
Does the price include fabric?+
All our standard service prices include fabric from our core collection. If you choose a premium designer fabric from our extended range, we'll quote the additional fabric cost separately — always clearly itemised.
Do you charge for the initial consultation?+
No — consultations are completely free of charge, whether in our studio or at your home. We believe in earning your business through expertise and honesty, not through commitment fees.
What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept BACS bank transfer, all major credit/debit cards and cheque. A 30% deposit is required when confirming your order, with the balance due upon delivery. Finance options are available for orders over £1,000.
