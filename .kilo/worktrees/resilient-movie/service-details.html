FAQ

Sofa Reupholstery Questions

Which sofa styles can you reupholster?+
We work with all sofa styles — Chesterfields, Knole, wing-back, modern sectionals, club sofas, Camelback and more. If it has a frame, we can restore it.
My sofa has deep buttoning — can you replicate it?+
Yes. Deep buttoning is one of our signature skills. We create a new diamond tufting pattern through the foam and fabric. If you want to change the button spacing or add tufting to a plain sofa, we can do this too.
Can I change the sofa's colour completely?+
Absolutely. That's the most common request! The sky's the limit — from a complete colour transformation to a subtle fabric upgrade. We have over 500 fabric options to choose from.
What if the frame needs major repair?+
We assess the frame at the start and report any issues before proceeding. Minor repairs are included in the service price. For major structural repairs, we'll provide a separate itemised quote for your approval.
