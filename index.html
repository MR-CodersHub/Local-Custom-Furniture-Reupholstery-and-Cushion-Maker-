Our Story

A Family Legacy Built on Fabric & Passion

Founded in 1987 by master upholsterer Thomas Mercer, Craftwork Atelier has grown from a small workshop to London's most trusted bespoke upholstery studio — whilst keeping every tradition and value intact.

We believe furniture holds memory. A grandmother's armchair, a wedding-gift sofa, a bench passed down through generations — these pieces deserve more than replacement. They deserve revival. Our artisans treat every piece as if it were their own, applying traditional hand-tying techniques alongside modern precision.

Thomas Mercer
Founder & Master Upholsterer

How It Works

Your Journey from
Concept to Masterpiece

Consultation & Assessment

We begin with a complimentary home or studio consultation. Our artisans assess your furniture's structure, advise on the best restoration approach and help you explore fabric options from our curated collection.

01
02

Fabric Selection & Quote

Browse our collection of over 500 premium fabrics with fabric samples sent directly to you. We provide a detailed, transparent quote with no hidden charges — and collect your piece for free within London.

Expert Restoration

Your piece is stripped to its frame, assessed for structural repairs, re-webbed and re-sprung where needed. New padding is applied before your chosen fabric is stretched and hand-tacked with precision.

03
04

Quality Check & Delivery

Every piece undergoes a rigorous 25-point quality inspection before being carefully wrapped and delivered back to your home. We handle white-glove installation at no extra cost.
