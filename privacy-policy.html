1. Introduction

Craftwork Atelier ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, request a quote, book a consultation, visit our Notting Hill studio, or use our reupholstery and custom cushion services.

We process personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

3. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide our services and improve your experience:

a) Information you provide directly

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number, billing and delivery address.
  • Project details: descriptions of your furniture, measurements, fabric preferences, budget, and photos you upload via our contact or quote forms.
  • Booking information: preferred consultation dates, studio visit appointments, collection and delivery instructions.
  • Account information: if you register or log in — username, email and encrypted password.
  • Newsletter subscription: your email address when you subscribe to our inspiration letters and offers.
  • Feedback: testimonials, reviews and survey responses you choose to share.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent, referring pages, and interactions (collected in anonymised / aggregated form where possible).
  • Device data: browser type, operating system and approximate location derived from IP address.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data (such as health or biometric data). Please do not include such information in your messages or photos.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data only for the following purposes, under the lawful bases stated:

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes — performance of pre-contract steps / legitimate interest.
  • To deliver our services — arranging consultations, collections, restoration work and deliveries — performance of a contract.
  • To process payments, deposits and invoices — performance of a contract and legal obligation.
  • To send service updates (booking confirmations, progress photos, delivery scheduling) — performance of a contract.
  • To send our newsletter and fabric inspiration — only with your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email.
  • To improve our website and services — legitimate interest in understanding how visitors use our site.
  • To comply with legal obligations — tax, accounting and warranty records.
We will never sell your personal data to third parties, and we will never use your photos or project details for marketing without your explicit permission.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses a small number of cookies to function correctly and to understand aggregate usage:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: remember your theme (light/dark), RTL preference, and keep forms working. These cannot be disabled without affecting the site.
  • Preference cookies: remember gallery filters and recently viewed fabrics.
  • Analytics (if enabled): anonymised page-view statistics to help us improve content. No cross-site advertising trackers are used.

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect features such as the quote form, theme toggle and image lightbox.

6. Sharing Your Data

We share personal data only when strictly necessary:

  • Fabric suppliers & couriers: name, address and phone number to order materials or arrange collection/delivery.
  • Payment providers: to securely process deposits and balances. We never store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Professional advisers: accountants and legal advisers bound by confidentiality, where required by law.
  • Authorities: where we are legally obliged to disclose information (e.g. tax audit, court order).

All third parties are required to respect the security of your data and use it only for the instructed purpose.

7. Data Retention

  • Enquiries that don't proceed: deleted or anonymised within 12 months.
  • Client project records (quotes, invoices, before/after photos, guarantee records): retained for 7 years for tax and 5-year craftsmanship guarantee purposes.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: retained until you unsubscribe, then removed within 30 days.
  • Website analytics: aggregated and anonymised; raw logs deleted within 14 months.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure ("right to be forgotten") where there is no overriding legal reason to retain it.
  • Object to or restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Request data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for newsletter or marketing communications.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@craftworkatelier.com with the subject "Data Request". We will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

9. Security

We protect your data with appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) transmission, access controls limited to staff who need it, secure password storage, and regular backups. While no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, we work hard to safeguard your information and will notify you and the ICO of any notifiable breach as required by law.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top will always show the current version. Material changes will be highlighted on this page, and where appropriate, notified by email.
