FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your upholstery service.

How long does reupholstery take?+
Typical turnaround is 2–4 weeks depending on the complexity of the piece and current studio workload. We provide an exact timeline at the consultation stage and keep you updated throughout the process.
Is my furniture worth reupholstering?+
Generally, if the frame is solid and the piece has sentimental or quality value, reupholstery is absolutely worthwhile — and far more sustainable than buying new. We provide honest assessments at no charge.
Can I supply my own fabric?+
Yes, we can work with customer-supplied fabric. We'll advise on the required yardage and suitability before you purchase. We also stock over 500 premium fabrics if you prefer to choose from our collection.
Do you offer a collection service?+
Yes — free collection and delivery is included for all services within the M25. For properties outside this area, we can arrange courier or recommend trusted transport partners at cost price.
What does your 5-year guarantee cover?+
Our guarantee covers all craftsmanship — seams, piping, tufting and fabric application. It does not cover normal wear and tear, accidental damage or damage caused by improper cleaning. Fabric manufacturer warranties apply separately.
Which fabrics are best for homes with pets and children?+
We recommend stain-resistant performance weaves and treated linens for busy households — they resist spills, claws and daily wear while still feeling soft and luxurious. During your consultation we will show you child- and pet-friendly options matched to your style and budget.
