1. About Us

Craftwork Atelier, 14 Heritage Lane, Notting Hill, London W11 2QA ("we", "us", "our") is a bespoke furniture reupholstery and custom cushion making studio operating since 1987. Contact: hello@craftworkatelier.com, +44 (0)20 7946 0123.

These terms apply to all services ordered via our website, by email, by phone, or in person at our studio. They should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and any written quote we provide.

2. Our Services

We provide bespoke reupholstery (sofas, Chesterfields, armchairs, headboards, benches, ottomans, dining chairs), custom cushion making (scatter, box, bench, window seat and outdoor cushions), and associated restoration work including frame repair, re-webbing, re-springing and re-padding.

Every piece is hand-finished by our artisans. Because restoration involves natural materials and heritage frames, minor variations in finish compared to catalogue imagery are normal and part of genuine hand craftsmanship.

3. Quotes & Pricing

  • All initial consultations and written quotes are free and without obligation.
  • Quotes are based on the photos, measurements and condition described at the time. If hidden structural damage is discovered after strip-down, we will contact you with photographic evidence and a revised price before proceeding — you are never committed to unexpected extras.
  • Quotes are valid for 30 days from the date of issue, after which fabric prices or availability may have changed.
  • Guide prices on our Pricing page ("From £…") are starting points; your itemised written quote is the binding price (our Fixed Price Promise).
  • Prices include VAT where applicable and will be clearly itemised (labour, fabric, fillings, collection/delivery).

4. Deposits & Payment

  • To confirm a booking we require a 50% deposit, which also covers ordering your chosen fabric (cut fabrics are non-returnable to mills).
  • The remaining balance is due on satisfactory delivery / collection, before or at handover.
  • We accept bank transfer and major debit/credit cards. Details are provided on your invoice.
  • Custom cushions under £150 are payable in full at order.
  • Late payment beyond 14 days of the due date may incur statutory interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts legislation (for trade clients) or a reasonable administration reminder process for consumers.
Your deposit secures your slot in our workshop schedule and your fabric order. Fabric is ordered immediately on receipt of deposit so your project can start without delay.

5. Fabrics & Materials

  • We offer 500+ fabrics from trusted mills. We will advise honestly on suitability (martindale rub count, fire rating, suitability for pets/children, outdoor use).
  • We strongly recommend ordering free fabric samples and viewing them in your own light before confirming. Screen colours vary from physical cloth.
  • Natural fabrics (linen, cotton, wool) may show subtle slub, weave variation and fading in direct sunlight over time — this is characteristic, not a fault.
  • All domestic upholstery fabrics we supply meet UK Fire Safety (Furniture & Furnishings) Regulations. We provide compliant FR interliners and fillings as standard.
  • If a chosen fabric is discontinued by the mill before ordering, we will offer the closest alternatives or a full refund of any deposit paid for that fabric.

6. Collection, Timelines & Delivery

  • Free collection & delivery applies within the M25 for reupholstery projects. Outside the M25, courier collection is arranged at cost with your approval.
  • Typical lead times: custom cushions 1–2 weeks; armchairs 3–4 weeks; sofas 4–6 weeks; full room sets by arrangement. Your quote will state an estimated completion window.
  • Timelines are estimates, not guarantees — heritage restoration can reveal surprises, and fabric mills occasionally delay. We will keep you updated with progress photos and revised dates.
  • On delivery we offer white-glove placement in your room of choice, including packaging removal. Please ensure clear access; waiting time beyond 30 minutes due to access issues may be chargeable.
  • If you cannot accept delivery on the agreed date, we offer up to 14 days of free studio storage; thereafter reasonable storage charges may apply.

7. Customer's Own Material (COM)

You are welcome to supply your own fabric. Please note:

  • We will advise on quantity required (including pattern repeat allowance) but cannot accept responsibility for shortages, flaws, or unsuitability of COM.
  • COM must be delivered to our studio before your slot start date. Delays may move your booking.
  • Our 5-year craftsmanship guarantee still covers our workmanship, but not defects inherent to customer-supplied fabric.

8. Guarantee & Aftercare

  • Every reupholstery project carries our 5-year craftsmanship guarantee covering springs, webbing, stitching, frame repairs we undertook, and cushion construction under normal domestic use.
  • The guarantee does not cover fair wear and tear, accidental damage, pet damage, fading from direct sunlight, or failure to follow our care instructions.
  • Outdoor cushions carry a 2-year guarantee against construction defects (fabric fading performance follows the mill's own warranty, typically 5 years for Sunbrella-type fabrics).
  • If a guarantee issue arises, contact us with photos. We will inspect and repair or remake at our cost where the fault is ours.
  • Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

9. Changes & Cancellations

  • You may change your fabric choice before we order it at no charge. Once fabric is cut/ordered, fabric costs are non-refundable.
  • Cooling-off: consumers booking at a distance (online/phone) may cancel within 14 days of booking for a full refund, unless work has begun at your express request.
  • Cancellation after work has begun: deposit covers fabric and work completed to date; any balance for completed labour is payable. Your stripped frame and materials will be returned to you on request.
  • To cancel, email hello@craftworkatelier.com or call +44 (0)20 7946 0123.

10. Liability

  • We carry full goods-in-transit and workshop insurance for pieces in our care.
  • Pre-existing weaknesses (e.g. brittle antique frames, woodworm damage discovered on strip-down) will be documented and discussed; we are not liable for failures inherent to the original piece that cannot reasonably be reinforced.
  • Our total liability for any project is limited to the contract value of that project, except where liability cannot be limited by law (death, personal injury caused by negligence, fraud).
  • We are not liable for delays outside our reasonable control (mill shortages, courier strikes, extreme weather).

11. Photos & Intellectual Property

We will only photograph your piece for our portfolio, website or social media with your explicit written permission, and we will always credit you only as you wish (or anonymously). You may request removal of your images at any time.

Our formal complaints address is hello@craftworkatelier.com, 14 Heritage Lane, London W11 2QA.
